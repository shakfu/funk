-- funk-midi --no-sleep --cycle 2000 --dirt 57120 --script (tests/CMakeLists.txt):
-- Tidal's transitions (lib/stream.fk), given past cycle 0 (lib/tidal-dirt
-- checks each at cycle 0 against Tidal's own). xfade from cycle 2: the gain
-- of a falls as b's rises, over 4 cycles; the slot then has no _id_, as in
-- Tidal. jump on a new slot, 2: c from cycle 6. A muted slot stays muted
-- through a transition. anticipateIn: a's echoes speed up over 1 cycle,
-- then d.
d1 $ s "a*2"
:wait 3990
xfade 1 $ s "b*2"
:wait 4000
jump 2 $ s "c"
mute 2
:wait 2000
jumpIn 2 0 $ s "e"
:wait 2000
unmute 2
d1 $ s "a*2"
:wait 1990
anticipateIn 1 1 $ s "d"
:wait 3000
list
